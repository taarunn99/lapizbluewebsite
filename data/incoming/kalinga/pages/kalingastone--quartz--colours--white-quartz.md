# White Quartz slabs in the UAE.

White engineered quartz is the most specified surface colour in the UAE, and the deepest part of the KalingaStone range: crisp Bianco whites, warm off-whites and crystal-fine particulates across every series tier. All slabs are 20 mm, non-porous and NSF-certified food safe, stocked in Sharjah by Global Classic for supply across the Emirates.
